# Getting Started

## Basic Navigation

The basics are a screen reader's. Arrows move focus. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle control groups. Enter activates. Backspace is the secondary action where a control has one, usually its details (the tooltip the game shows on hover). Escape backs out. Home and End jump to the ends of a list, Alt+Up and Alt+Down to the previous or next section. Typing letters searches the focused group by name.

Focus announcements are deliberately short: name, value, state, role. Everything longer a control carries, its tooltip, keyword explanations, what it affects, waits in the buffers; see [Buffers](buffers.md).

## Keys Here

**Press F1, anywhere.** It lists the keys that do something right where you are, the most particular first: the board keys in an encounter, and what Escape does on this screen. Enter on a row runs that key for you: you hear whatever the key says, then the control you were on.

That list is the reference, and it is always current. [Controls](controls.md) is the full list for when you want to see everything at once.

## The Encounter

An encounter has two control groups; Tab moves between them.

- The board. Its top row is the column headers: each column's Dice Power and the perk in its slot. Below it, the grid, top row first; a cell reads its coordinates, column then row, with row 1 at the bottom, and the unit on it. Under the grid is whatever the turn is at: your dice and Launch while you roll, the shop's choices and Proceed in the shop, the targets while the game asks you to pick one. Below that, the reroll, budge, and Mini-Dice buttons.
- The status: the target, your attack against it, turns left, hearts, tokens, Mult, then your perks.

A die's face names the column it scores. The digits 1 to 6 read that column whole from anywhere in the encounter: its header, then its cells top first. Shift with a digit goes there.

When you launch, the scoring is said column by column as the game plays it, with what each column set off, then the turn's total. Everything else the turn changed follows once the board settles.

The glance keys (Ctrl+A attack, Ctrl+T turns and hearts, and the rest under [Controls](controls.md#in-a-run)) read a figure without moving focus, over the game menu and the result screens too. Escape opens the game menu; Ctrl+S opens it on the game's stats overlay.

## The Mod Menu

Ctrl+Shift+M opens it. Settings has two switches: Speak list positions, and Keyboard navigation on at launch. All keys lists every key the mod binds, by group.
