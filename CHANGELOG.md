# Changelog

## V0.0.7

- Units with negative strength are now properly handled. Beyond me why they wouldn't just put a - and have a separate flag.
- A unit destroyed by its own scoring, such as Puppet, now counts in its column's attack.
- The Oculus issue is finally put to rest.
- On the commander select, the unique artifacts should now be in the order they unlock.

## V0.0.6

- On the commander select, the chapter's or challenge's modifiers are now read.
- The chapter panel now reads its "New Mechanic" line and, for daily challenges, when the daily mods reset.
- The current mission now names the commanders you have already won its chapter with.
- Chapter 6's missions now say which are completed.
- The mission area is now read on every profile.
- Descriptions no longer start with a stray dash where the game draws a bullet.

## V0.0.5

- New, hopefully less verbose scoring mode, is available to try in mod settings.
- The end of a turn no longer repeats a Mult that the turn left unchanged.
- After picking a unit to swap or move, focus now stays on that unit's cell.
- The Reroll button now shows under a pilot's rewards, and rerolling the legendary draft says the new choices.
- In effect descriptions, the trigger arrow now has a comma before it, so the condition and the effect are easier to tell apart.
- A Chrono Rift face on a die is now read as "Chrono Rift".
- On the commander select, a commander's unique artifacts are now one list.
- Toggles in the mod's settings no longer say their new state twice.

## V0.0.4

- The Warped Oculus now says it is looking left. It said "looking right", though it affects the units to its left.
- An Oculus's facing is now in its details (Backspace), after its description, instead of on its cell.

## V0.0.3

- We now properly read curse modifiers.
- Some astral items included a stray = in their descriptions. This has been fixed.

## V0.0.2

- Kraken dice are now read properly.
- In pilot fights, Ctrl+O also reads the pilot's ability.
- Skip remaining turns now says how much shop rarity skipping gives.
- With the Overseer chosen, the commander select now reads its two borrowed commanders, a row each with that commander's perk after it, and lets you pick them.
